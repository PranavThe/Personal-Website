    XIThe sound post

    L'âme

    Inside, a spruce post six millimeters wide stands between the top and the back. Nothing glues it in. Pressure holds it. Move it a millimeter and the whole voice changes. The French call it l'âme and the Italians call it anima: the soul.

    Mine is the people. My parents, my mentors in the lab and in music, my roommates, and the friends I play volleyball with.
